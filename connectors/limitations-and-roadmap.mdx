Connectors are still being built. Today, the only Connector is the Iroh-powered [Tunnel](/connectors/tunnels), and its first use case is the [Desktop Apps](/desktop-apps).

## Planned capabilities

- **"Anywhere to anywhere" tunnels**, beyond exposing a local environment to the internet.
- **Developer connections**, such as Tailscale tailnets and WireGuard VPNs.
- **Telco connections**, such as AWS Direct Connect, Equinix Fabric, and Megaport onramps.

For what Datum is building next across the platform, see the [Platform roadmap](/platform/roadmap).

<Note>
  Plans can change. This list isn't a commitment to deliver any feature or to meet any timeline.
</Note>
