Connectors are how you bring connections from outside Datum into the Datum network. Datum is still building Connectors, and documentation will grow with the product.

## Available today

- **[Tunnels](/connectors/tunnels):** flexible QUIC tunnels powered by the Iroh protocol. The first use case is the [Desktop Apps](/desktop-apps), which securely expose a local environment to the internet through an [Application Load Balancer](/alb/overview).

## Planned

- **Developer connections:** such as Tailscale tailnets and WireGuard VPNs.
- **Telco connections:** such as AWS Direct Connect, Equinix Fabric, and Megaport onramps.
- **"Anywhere to anywhere" tunnels.**

See [Limitations and roadmap](/connectors/limitations-and-roadmap) for details.
