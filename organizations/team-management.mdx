To collaborate with team members, you create or join an organization. This page will describe how to invite team members, change their roles, and remove them.

## Roles

Organizations support the following coarse-grained roles:

- **Owners** - Full control over the organization and all resources.
- **Editors** - Control over all resources in an organization, except for team members.
- **Viewers** - Read-only access to all resources in an organization.

## Invitation expiry

An invitation to join an organization expires seven days after it's sent. This gives someone new to Datum time to create an account before they accept.

On your organization's **Team** page, each pending invitation shows when it expires, for example "Expires in 6 days". Hover over it to see the exact time. Once an invitation expires, its badge changes from **Invited** to **Expired**. To invite that person again, select **Resend** on their row.

If you open an invitation link that has expired, Datum Cloud shows **This invitation has expired**. Ask the person who invited you to send a new invitation. If the invitation may have gone to a different email address, the page shows the address you're signed in with and a link to sign out, so you can sign in with the invited address instead.
