An organization is the top level of Datum's data model. It holds your [Projects](/projects/overview), the team members who can work in them, your payment method, and the quotas that apply across them.

This page will describe how to create and manage organizations, and how they relate to Projects.

- [Team management](/organizations/team-management) — the Owner, Editor and Viewer roles. Steps for inviting members are coming soon.
- [Usage](/organizations/usage) — see what your organization uses and how it compares to its quotas.
