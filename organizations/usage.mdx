Datum is in active development and currently only offers a free tier with reasonable limits on resources. Resource limits (quotas) can be found under the settings for Organizations and [Projects](/projects/overview).

## See your usage

To see your organization's usage in the [Cloud Portal](https://cloud.datum.net), select your organization, then select **Usage**. The page shows metered consumption across every project in the organization for the current billing cycle. To look at an earlier cycle or a single project, use the filters at the top.

Usage is grouped by product. For example, Compute meters the CPU and memory allocated to your Instances and how long they run, and DNS meters active records and the queries Datum answers.
