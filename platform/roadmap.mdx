Datum is still being built. We are building a public cloud that is free during public beta, with usage-based [pricing](https://www.datum.net/pricing), and working closely with design partners on managed cloud and BYOC approaches. This page summarizes where each product stands.

## Available today

Our public cloud is opinionated, but powered by key partners. We call these our "house brand" services:

- **[Application Load Balancer](/alb/overview):** an [Envoy](/platform/envoy) edge proxy and AI gateway, with a built-in [web application firewall](/alb/configuration/waf).
- **[Connectors](/connectors/overview):** a way to orchestrate connections, starting with QUIC tunnels powered by [Iroh](/platform/iroh-relays).
- **[Domains and DNS](/domain-dns/overview):** domain tracking and verification, plus global authoritative DNS.
- **Essentials:** [metrics export](/operations/metrics-export), [activity logs](/operations/activity-logs), [secrets](/operations/secrets), and team collaboration.

## In preview

- **[Compute](/compute/overview):** isolated Instances that run container images or fast-starting [unikernels](/compute/runtimes/unikernels) in Datum locations.
- **[gVPC](/galactic-vpc/overview):** private IPv6 networks for your Instances that span every location they run in, on Datum's [SRv6](/platform/segment-routing) backbone.

## What we're working on

- **More Connectors:** developer options such as Tailscale tailnets and WireGuard VPNs, and telco options such as AWS Direct Connect, Equinix Fabric, and Megaport onramps.
- **vMMR:** a virtual "meet me room" for interacting with diverse connections.
- **Networking for Instances:** firewall rules and private DNS. See [gVPC planned capabilities](/galactic-vpc/limitations-and-roadmap#planned-capabilities).

## Follow along and get involved

The best place to track our roadmap is our [website and milestones](https://www.datum.net/roadmap/). To suggest a feature, visit our [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/orgs/datum-cloud/discussions/categories/feature-requests). For more ways to take part, see [Get involved](/get-involved).

### Enhancement proposals

Datum designs major changes in the open, in the [datum-cloud/enhancements](https://github.com/datum-cloud/enhancements) repository on GitHub. It holds proposals for significant architectural changes, cross-cutting work such as security and performance, and long-term roadmap items that span several releases. Each proposal starts as an issue, is refined with maintainers and the community, and becomes an enhancement document. Proposals move from `Backlog` to `In Progress` to `Completed`.

To follow the design behind what's coming, browse the [open enhancements](https://github.com/datum-cloud/enhancements/issues). To propose one of your own, open a new issue there. For smaller ideas and feature requests, use [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/orgs/datum-cloud/discussions/categories/feature-requests) instead.
