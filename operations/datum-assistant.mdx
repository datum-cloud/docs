---
title: "Datum Assistant"
description: "Ask Patch, the Datum Cloud assistant, about your resources in natural language, and let it diagnose problems for you."
---

Patch uses diagnostic tools from the Datum services your project uses, so it can work out why a Compute workload isn't available, why a load balancer isn't serving, or why a network isn't ready. It never changes anything on its own: it proposes a change as a plan, shows you the exact manifests, and applies them only when you approve.

Patch is available in the terminal with `datumctl assistant`. Conversations are kept by Datum, so you can pick one up from any machine you're signed in on.

```bash
datumctl plugin install assistant
datumctl assistant chat "Why is the api-backend workload not available?"
```

Usage is metered by messages and tokens. See your organization's [Usage](/organizations/usage).
