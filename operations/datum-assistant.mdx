---
title: "Datum Assistant"
description: "Ask Datum about your resources in natural language."
---

The Datum Assistant translates natural language into Datum Cloud operations. Ask it a question, such as "list all DNS zones" or "which projects am I in?", and it works out which resources to read and shows you the answer. Before it changes anything, it asks you to confirm.

The assistant is available today in the CLI as `datumctl ai`. You supply your own model API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Gemini), and your key and requests go directly to the provider you choose.
