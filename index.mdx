**Datum is an open and neutral network built for the AI era and focused on builders and service providers.**

Datum unlocks internet superpowers — advanced network and connectivity services — for every agent, app, and builder. Unlike a traditional CDN or hyperscaler cloud:

- We are 100% focused on builders and modern, "alt cloud" service providers.
- Our code is open source (AGPLv3), so you can run it anywhere if needed.
- We prioritize agent-first and developer experiences.

Datum is still being built. The product is evolving quickly, documentation is growing with it, and today we offer a free tier. To see where each product stands, check the [Roadmap](/platform/roadmap).

## Why Datum

Our platform provides internet-scale primitives — intelligent edge proxies, private backbones, cloud onramps, peering, meet-me rooms — with an experience that is 100% programmatic, agent-first, and backed by AGPLv3 open source.

We believe a network in the AI era is different from a traditional CDN, mainly because the use case is intelligence, not caching. We also think that open — open source and open ecosystems — is a critical requirement in a world that is increasingly fragmented and regulated.

We serve builders and modern service providers, which we lovingly call [Alt Clouds](https://www.alt-cloud.org/). We aim to support thousands of them, so neutrality and transparency are important in everything we do. We started Datum because we saw three big trends:

1. Intelligence and data everywhere
2. Thousands of new providers
3. An era of increased regulation
