sidebarTitle: "Overview"

---

Datum treats domains and DNS zones as resources in your project, managed through the Cloud Portal, `datumctl`, or [Datum MCP](/agents/datum-mcp).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Domains" icon="globe" href="/domain-dns/domains">
    Track domains wherever they are registered, and verify ownership to use them with other Datum services.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DNS" icon="server" href="/domain-dns/dns">
    Host authoritative DNS for your zones on Datum's global anycast network.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Domains and DNS work together with the [Application Load Balancer](/alb/overview): a verified domain can serve traffic on a load balancer, and a Datum-hosted zone can point at it. See [DNS setup for Application Load Balancer](/guides/domain-dns/alb-dns-setup).
