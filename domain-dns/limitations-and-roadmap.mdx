Domains and DNS are still being built. This page lists known limitations.

## Limitations

- **DNSSEC isn't supported.** Zones hosted on Datum aren't signed. Turn DNSSEC off at your registrar before you move a domain to Datum. See [DNSSEC](/domain-dns/dns#dnssec).

For what Datum is building next across the platform, see the [Platform roadmap](/platform/roadmap).
