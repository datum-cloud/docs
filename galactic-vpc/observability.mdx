This page will cover how to see what your networks are doing, such as the traffic between Instances and the health of the paths between locations.

## In the meantime

- To see each Instance's private address, run `datumctl compute instances --workload=WORKLOAD_NAME`. See [Addressing](/galactic-vpc/networks/addressing).
- To view metrics and logs for the workloads on a network, see [Compute observability](/compute/observability).
