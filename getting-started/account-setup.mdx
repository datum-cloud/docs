The first step is to create a user account at [https://cloud.datum.net](https://cloud.datum.net). You can sign up with Google or GitHub, or with your email address and a passkey. For the steps, see [Create an account](/account/create-account).

Once you’ve created a user account, you can personalize your experience and access details by selecting your avatar or initials at the top right of your screen.

## Orgs & Projects

We use a standard cloud data model that associates [Projects](/projects/overview) within [Organizations](/organizations/overview). All organizations use our **standard** quota limits, including support for collaborators, projects, and shared resources.

When you finish signing up, Datum creates your first organization and your first project for you. You can rename them, or create more, at any time.

## Payment method

A payment method is required for every active organization to improve the security and stability of our platform.

Datum is currently free during public beta, and your account won’t accrue any charges without prior notice. For how usage will be priced, see [pricing](https://www.datum.net/pricing).

### Confirming your contact details

As part of adding your payment method, we’ll ask you to confirm:

- Your **legal name**.
- Any **company name** you want associated with an organization.

## Team management

To collaborate with team members, you’ll need to create or join an Organization. Organizations support the following coarse-grained roles:

- **Owners** - Full control over the organization and all resources.
- **Editors** - Control over all resources in an organization, except for team members.
- **Viewers** - Read-only access to all resources in an organization.

For more information, see [Team management](/organizations/team-management).

## Quotas

Datum is in active development and is free during public beta, with reasonable limits on resources. Resource limits (e.g. quotas) can be found under the setting areas for Organizations and Projects. For more information, see [Usage](/organizations/usage).

## Support

If you run into a problem, including a quota that limits your work, email [support@datum.net](mailto:support@datum.net), or use the live chat at the top of the portal. [Discord](https://link.datum.net/discord) is also an option!
